# Supply Chain Disruption Control Agent

## Overview
Global supply chains face frequent disruptions due to extreme weather, geopolitical port congestion, and unexpected supplier outages. Traditional ERP/SCM platforms rely on manual human intervention to recalculate routes and reallocate inventory, resulting in high SLA penalties and revenue loss.

Your objective is to build an intelligent, autonomous agent system that continuously monitors incoming disruption feeds, evaluates constraint impacts across logistics nodes, generates optimal remediation workflows, and executes dynamic re-routing decisions with full audit transparency.

## Core Capabilities Required

### 1. Disruption Ingestion & Triage
- Ingest real-time alert events (port closures, weather anomalies, carrier delays, supplier downtime).
- Classify severity (Low, Medium, High, Critical) and determine impacted orders, inventory batches, and shipment corridors.

### 2. Multi-Modal Re-Routing & Optimization
- Evaluate alternate transport modes (Air, Sea, Rail, Road) against SLA delivery windows and cost constraints.
- Re-allocate warehouse inventory dynamically when primary supply nodes fail.

### 3. Tool Execution & Constraint Validation
- Use agent tools / function calls to query inventory APIs, route distance estimators, and carrier rate tables.
- Enforce strict constraints (e.g., maximum budget overhead, hazardous material transport rules, customer priority tiers).

### 4. Audit Trail & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards
- Output human-readable reasoning logs explaining why a specific mitigation route was chosen over alternatives.
- Flag high-cost (> $50,000 impact) or high-risk decisions for explicit human coordinator approval.

## Deliverables & Scoring Criteria
- Autonomous Decision Accuracy & Constraint Compliance: 40%
- System Architecture, Tool Design & Agent Robustness: 30%
